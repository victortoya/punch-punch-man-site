Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Punch Punch Man ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use the game on your iOS device. We are committed to being transparent about our practices.

1. Information We Collect

Punch Punch Man does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) from its users. Specifically:

  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, location, or any other personal data.
  • Your high score is stored locally on your device using Apple's UserDefaults and never sent to any server.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect certain information automatically through the AdMob SDK, including but not limited to:

  • Advertising ID (IDFA / GAID) — subject to your device permissions and ATT consent
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • Approximate location (based on IP address)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google under their own privacy policy. You can review Google's privacy practices at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You may opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings under Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.

3. App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On iOS 14 and later, the App will request your permission before tracking your data across apps and websites. You may choose to allow or decline tracking. Declining will not affect core gameplay — you may still see non-personalized ads.

4. Children's Privacy

Punch Punch Man is rated for general audiences. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

5. Third-Party Links

The App may display ads that link to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any third-party site you visit.

6. Data Retention

Since we do not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our end. Your local high score data remains on your device and can be cleared by deleting the App.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
