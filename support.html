How to Play

Punch Punch Man is a one-tap arcade action game. Here's how it works:

  • Villains appear at the top of the screen and march toward your hero across three lanes: Left, Center, and Right.
  • Tap the PUNCH button that matches the villain's lane to knock them out.
  • Let even one villain reach your hero and it's Game Over.
  • Your score goes up with every successful hit. Beat your personal best!

Frequently Asked Questions

My high score was reset. What happened?

High scores are stored locally on your device. If you deleted and reinstalled the App, or reset your device's settings, the score may have been cleared. Unfortunately, cloud backup for scores is not available in this version.

The game is not launching or keeps crashing.

Try closing the App fully and reopening it. If the issue persists, restart your device. Make sure your iOS is updated to the latest version. If you still experience crashes, please contact us with your device model and iOS version.

There is no sound or the music stopped.

Check that your device is not on Silent mode (flip the mute switch on the side of your iPhone). Also make sure the volume is turned up. If the issue continues, try closing and reopening the App.

I'm seeing ads. How can I opt out of personalized ads?

You can opt out of personalized advertising by going to Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising and turning off "Personalized Ads". You will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized.

When will more heroes be available?

Additional heroes are planned for a future update. Stay tuned — the "Coming Soon" slots in the hero selection screen will be unlocked in an upcoming version.

How do I delete my data?

All data (your high score) is stored locally on your device. To delete it, simply delete the App from your device. We do not store any personal data on our servers.
